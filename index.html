ABOUT

Understanding psychiatric
disorders through genetics.

My primary research focus is psychiatric genetics. I investigate the genetic architecture and underlying biology of psychiatric disorders.

I am a PhD student in the Genomics & Digital Health Laboratory at Sungkyunkwan University. My broader interests include multi-omics integration, therapeutic target prioritization, Alzheimer’s disease genetics, and complex trait genetics.
